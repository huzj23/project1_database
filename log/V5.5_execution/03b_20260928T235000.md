# V5.5 stage 03 report — collision proxies and the static support chain

Run id: `03_20260928T204500` (extended). Supersedes nothing; the earlier 03 record is kept and
this report states exactly which of its conclusions changed and why.

## What stage 03 had to establish

03 §4–§5 require, for every dynamic prop and every static surface the props touch:

* a collision proxy whose dimensions match the visual within 1 %, and whose contact face
  deviates by no more than `min(2 mm, thinnest effective thickness × 5 %)`;
* concave geometry represented as convex parts rather than as one filled hull, because a body
  must be able to ENTER a cup rather than rest on its mouth;
* every kerb a dynamic body can sweep into present in collision;
* static collision extracted from the real scene, with the original static instance hidden and
  archived rather than deleted.

## The two silent failures that blocked this stage

Both produced a body falling with **zero contacts** while every other signal looked normal, and
neither could be found from the trajectory. Both were located by isolating one variable at a
time.

### 1. The `vertices=`/`indices=` collision-shape route does not collide

`createCollisionShape(GEOM_MESH, vertices=…, indices=…)` builds a shape that does not collide
with `GEOM_PLANE` or with a concave trimesh in this PyBullet build (API 202010061, built Jan
2025). The `fileName=` route works correctly.

Proven with the strongest available control — the **same** 20 mm cube mesh, the **same** plane,
changing only the creation route (`outcomes/v55/scenes/italian_flat/route_root_cause.json`):

| route | settled z | contacts | result |
|---|---|---|---|
| `vertices=` / `indices=` | −4.048 | 0 | fell through |
| `fileName=` | 0.5110 | 4 | rests |

This also corrects an earlier conclusion of mine, which I had written up as "GEOM_MESH does not
collide with GEOM_PLANE in this PyBullet build". That was a misdiagnosis: **meshes collide with
planes fine when loaded from a file**, and the shape pair was never the variable. It also
explains why all 36 engine-parameter combinations failed — every one of them used the broken
route — and why the control box and sphere rested, since primitives never use it.

### 2. A static `GEOM_MESH` is silently convex-hulled unless flagged concave

The Italian Flat props stand on the tray `Vassoio`, measured by mesh raycast as an open shell
with its **floor at z = 0.510600** and its **rim at z = 0.522260** (11.66 mm kerb). Handed to
PyBullet unflagged, that dish collides as its convex hull — a solid block whose top is the rim
plane — and a control probe came to rest at z = 0.523247, **12.65 mm above the real floor**.
Every prop then reported ~13.3 mm of initial penetration, far outside 04's cap of
`min(1 mm, t_min × 5 %)`.

Flagging the collider `GEOM_FORCE_CONCAVE_TRIMESH` removes the error entirely. Candidate
comparison, each measured in its own fresh world (`tray_collider_final.json`):

| static tray collider | probe support z | bias vs measured floor | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| A mesh, no flags | 0.523247 | **+12.647 mm** | fail |
| B mesh + `GEOM_FORCE_CONCAVE_TRIMESH` | 0.510590 | **−0.0104 mm** | **chosen** |
| C V-HACD compound, 71 convex parts | 0.511868 | +1.268 mm | passes (worse) |
| D synthetic box floor + rim walls | 0.510589 | −0.0106 mm | passes |

The exact authored mesh with the flag wins, so the tray needs **no approximation at all**. The
V-HACD compound (C) is retained as a documented fallback: it also passes, but only within
+1.268 mm, and raising its resolution does not help (coarse +2.255, default +1.268, fine
+1.866, very fine +1.886 mm) — the residual is voxel quantisation of a 22.26 mm shallow dish,
not a tuning failure.

## Final acceptance

`outcomes/v55/scenes/italian_flat/proxy_acceptance_final.json` — **PASS**.

Static support: concave tray support z median **0.510590**, bias **−0.0104 mm** against the
2 mm bound, floor level (max probe drift 0.0361 mm/s).

| prop | sealed | chosen proxy | triangles | initial penetration | bias vs floor | vs visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `bottle_assembly` | yes | `hull` | 412 | 0.0000 mm | +0.593 mm | 2.7e−08 mm |
| `glass_a` | no | `vhacd` | 994 | 0.0000 mm | +0.990 mm | 1.0e−05 mm |
| `glass_b` | no | `vhacd` | 1042 | 0.0000 mm | +0.990 mm | 1.3e−05 mm |

Two decisions are 03's own rules rather than convenience:

* the **sealed** bottle uses one closed convex proxy, which 03 §4 explicitly permits
  ("prefer to enable only one reliable sealed/capped bottle this round") — and the stage-05
  raycast independently confirmed the cap `Tappo Cristallo` sits on the bottle top with a
  **0 mm gap**, so the assembly is genuinely sealed;
* the **open cups** use only the V-HACD decomposition. 03 §4 forbids filling an open cup with a
  single closed hull, because a body could rest on the mouth instead of entering it. The `hull`
  candidate for each cup is measured and reported, but is **not** eligible, and it is also the
  candidate that fails the rest criterion — so the rule and the measurement agree.

Each proxy reproduces its own visual reference to within 1.3e−05 mm, i.e. the proxy is not a
loose approximation of the visual but the same surface.

## Placement: zero initial penetration, engine-verified

Each body's spawn height is solved against PyBullet's own narrowphase until the reported initial
penetration is ≤ 0.1 mm — in practice 2 iterations. This is a **geometric placement correction
that the engine verifies**: no velocity, force, or constraint is ever applied to any body, and
every body settles under gravity alone. The trace of every iteration is recorded in
`penetration_trace_m`.

## Solver and contract changes carried into stage 04

* `StaticCollider` gained `concave: bool` and `support_z_m: float | None`. The flag is part of
  the collider's **identity**, because without it the recorded geometry is not the geometry that
  collides; `support_z_m` records the independently raycast-measured support height so it can be
  checked against the collider rather than trusted.
* `MultibodySolver._create_static` now always passes `planeNormal=[0,0,1]` (a plane created
  without it is not horizontal) and applies the concave flag.
* `MultibodySolver.self_check()` was added. It drops a control box on every static collider and
  requires it to be supported; for a declared-concave collider it requires the probe to rest
  **below** the collider's bounding-box top; and where `support_z_m` is recorded it requires the
  probe to rest at that height within 2 mm. The scene's dynamic bodies are masked during the
  check so their own fall cannot contaminate the probe — an earlier version of this check
  reported the dish as unsupported for exactly that reason.

`outcomes/v55/scenes/italian_flat/self_check_effectiveness.json` proves the check works:

| scenario | `self_check` | why |
|---|---|---|
| plane | `ok=True` | control box rests |
| tray declared concave but **unflagged** | `ok=False` | probe rests at 0.523247, **12.647 mm** from the recorded 0.510600 |
| tray correctly flagged | `ok=True` | probe rests at 0.510590, error **0.0100 mm** |
| `vertices=`/`indices=` route on a plane | falls through | limitation still reproducible, so it cannot silently return |

The mis-declaration case matters because no flag-consistency check can see it: there the
declaration itself said "convex". It is caught only by checking the collider against an
**independent measurement**, which is why `support_z_m` exists.

Stage 04 remains **22/22 PASS** after these changes.

## Layer build, corrected

`tools/v55_build_layers.py` now routes soft furnishings to the visual/background layer instead
of into static collision. The first build had put `Sofa`, `Sofa.001`, `Sofa.005` and `Pillows`
into `environment_static_collision` merely because they fell inside the reach box — a rigid body
colliding with a rigid proxy of something the source treats as soft is exactly the unsupported
soft-body simulation this project excludes. They are matched by keyword, kept visible, and their
size and AABB recorded in `soft_background` so stage 05's no-go check can be enforced against a
number. Result: **4 static collision objects** (Table 1372 tri, Table.001 4134 tri, Vassoio
2772 tri, lamp 11046 tri), 4 soft background-only objects, 13 lights preserved, 464 objects
archived and hidden, source never overwritten.

Per-object collision meshes are exported alongside the merged one because the merged region has
**283816 triangles**, and the `vertices=`/`indices=` route fails above roughly 100 000 triangles
(measured: 100 000 OK, 200 000 FAILED). Every collider is therefore small enough to load by
either route.

## Disclosures that must travel with the scene

1. Bodies are spawned with the engine-verified zero-penetration placement above; **no velocity,
   force, or constraint is applied to any body**.
2. **Box and prop deformation is not simulated** — all bodies are rigid. This is a known
   pipeline limitation and is disclosed rather than modelled.
3. The static tray collider is the exact authored mesh with `GEOM_FORCE_CONCAVE_TRIMESH`;
   without the flag PyBullet hulls it and props float 12.65 mm high.
4. All mesh colliders are created with `fileName=` because the `vertices=`/`indices=` route
   produces non-colliding shapes against `GEOM_PLANE` and concave trimeshes in this build.
5. This PyBullet build exposes **no collision-margin API** (`collisionMargin`/`margin` are
   rejected on both `GEOM_BOX` and `GEOM_MESH`; `getPhysicsEngineParameters()` has no margin
   key), so **no margin is set and none is claimed**; correctness rests on closed geometry plus
   the zero-penetration placement above.
6. Soft furnishings (Sofa, Sofa.001, Sofa.005, Pillows) are **background only** and are excluded
   from the dynamic collision world.
