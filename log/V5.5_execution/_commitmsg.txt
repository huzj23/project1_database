V5.5 stage 08: measure domino box candidates; stage 05 progress and issue records

Stage 08 box selection. All 138 GSO assets with geometry were measured; 38 satisfy section 2's
height/thickness and width/thickness bands and have a collision proxy. Those 38 plus two controls
were then checked for the property a bounding box cannot establish -- whether the object is a
closed, flat-bottomed box that can stand and topple as a unit. Exactly one asset qualifies:
Hasbro_Cranium_Performance_and_Acting_Game at 272.6 x 55.8 x 207.7 mm, boxiness 0.989, base
footprint coverage 0.994, strike face 0.967, tipping angle 11.45 deg. A 12-box chain needs 1.12 m
of boxes and about 2.12 m of clear floor at a 0.15*h gap.

The measurement was wrong three times before it was right, and each failure reported "no asset is
usable" rather than "the metric is wrong". Attempt 1 used the base plane's share of total surface
area, whose ceiling for a cube is 1/6. Attempt 2 used its share of total projected area, whose
ceiling for a closed box is exactly 1/2 -- the best boxes measured 0.48-0.50, sitting on the
ceiling. Attempt 3 assumed the scan's stored axes were the box's axes, so a slightly rotated box
reported a strike-face coverage of 0.001 while a figurine reported 0.42. The final version recovers
the object's own frame from its face normals and chooses the standing orientation explicitly. All
four attempts and their tells are recorded in log/V5.5_execution/08_box_selection.md.

Two library facts that stage 08 must disclose rather than absorb: URDF masses are scan artefacts
(0.00006-0.00717 kg; a 273 mm game box is recorded as 0.0028 kg), and many assets are hollow, with
inertia 0.087-1.310 of the solid-box formula that section 3 prescribes.

Stage 05. Keyframe and full continuous preview passes are complete and the preview encodes and
verifies clean (H.264/yuv420p/1280x720/24fps, 62 frames, 2.5833 s; ffprobe, full decode, sampled
frames and a frame-to-frame change measurement taken from the ENCODED file, peak 3.21% at frames
7->8 which is the contact instant). The final 1920x1080/64 spp pass is rendering at a measured
702 s/frame with 8 threads; the 3-frame cost probe section 3 requires was taken first.
